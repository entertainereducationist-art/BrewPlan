BrewPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

BrewPlan is a campus café menu and pickup-ordering service for the NUST H-12 campus. This policy explains the information processed when you use the service.

Information we process

Depending on how you use BrewPlan, the service may process your name, email address, department, school, registration number, batch, phone number (if supplied), account role, café staff assignment, menu interactions, orders, pickup details and recipes you submit. Authentication credentials are handled by the authentication provider; BrewPlan does not display or store your plain-text password in its application tables.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, distinguish student and administrator access, display campus café menus, process pickup orders, support café staff fulfilment, manage submitted recipes, provide account support and protect the service.

Service providers and storage

BrewPlan uses Supabase for authentication and database services and Vercel for website hosting and delivery. These providers process information to operate the service under their respective terms and privacy policies. Data may be processed in the regions configured by these providers.

Payments

BrewPlan currently does not process online payments. Any payment arrangement made directly with a café is outside the app's payment processing.

Sharing

Order details may be made available to the relevant café staff so they can fulfil a pickup order. Account and operational information may be accessible to authorised BrewPlan administrators for service administration and support. BrewPlan does not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Information is retained while needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs and comply with applicable requirements. Access controls and database policies are used to restrict access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and account deletion

You may request access, correction or deletion of your account information by contacting the service operator. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, legal or operational reasons. Account deletion functionality should be made available in the app before release through app stores where required by their policies.

Children

BrewPlan is intended for university users and is not designed for children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published at this URL.
